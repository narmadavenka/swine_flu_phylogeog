# mcc_1990_v3.trees
	# created with the following parameters:strict molecular clock, exponential growth coalescent and a 300 million chain length, with sampling every 30,000 states (19). 
	# Convergence was confirmed by effective sample sizes (ESS) above 200 for all parameters apart from joint and coalescent, and a 10% burn-in was implemented when combining tree files and in extraction of the MCC tree

# H3_thinned_1000.trees
	# using the posterior tree distribution that generated mcc_1990_v3.trees, a minimum of 1000 trees were subsampled, focusing on nodes with posterior probabilities of at least 0.9 and picking the top 10% trees that supported genetic relationships between sequences
	# n=1020 trees were extracted that were used to generate phylogenetic uncertainty for the GLMs and hypothesized ecological drivers
